This is the biggest city on our way to [[Sevenarches]] - it is next to a rather large forest. This is the bandit city where the bandits and thieves guilds tend to kind of congregate. All of the crime happens outside of the city but this is basically a safe haven for thieves and bandits. There are thick walls surrounding the city and it is well-fortified.
