# Planning the Prison Break
We begin planning this out and [[Taraaq]] gives us some vague information about the city and what's going on. We need to get the people together and then escape into a portal to Sigil. Taraaq explains that Sigil is the city of portals and it's strange: it's easier to teleport to Sigil if you know what you're doing. It may be easier to find the portal to Sigil from the plane of water: there is bound to be at least one in the city, perhaps even in the palace itself. Taraaq explains that Sigil may look nice, but it's dangerous.

Zimri comments that we're already somewhere dangerous and Taraaq explains that Sigil is a different kind of dangerous: it's a 'train hub' (as Silver put it) that is run by a force that doesn't allow gods. They don't know what the Lady of Pain is, and Silver comments that she doesn't want to find out what that means. Achille says she'll need to be on her best behavior.

Taraaq says that if one of us gets maze'd, then they cannot save them. He explains that if the Lady of Pain is annoyed with you then you are put into a maze. The mazes are generally fair - you can find your way out, but sometimes they aren't.

There are wizards in Taraaq's group that can get us out, but it will take time. He asks if we should get people out or if we should find the portal first. There are six others that we need to get out.

Silver recommends we find the portal first and then bribe some people for proper passage or to otherwise look the other way while we make our way out. Once the portal is secured we grab the people and hurry out.

Pyrisou speaks up and explains that he is the best one for an extraction. Zimri comments that we should hear him out and he suggests we find the portal and then we convince the people around it to give it a wide breadth, and then he waits. His suggestion is to do this quietly: once we get there, the pirates are gone, and he burned a substantial favor on us. He explains that he gets to purge himself of the emotional baggage of feeling emotions over one of us. He comments he got over his crush after seeing Achille in this iteration.

We begin to speak and we find out from he pirates that they're aware that Taraaq has escaped and they're going to execute the prisoners. So we need to find the closest and easiest to get to portal area. We have to either do a subtle prisoner swap or a very blatant in your face hero rescue moment.
