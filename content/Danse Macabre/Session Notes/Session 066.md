# The Bank Robbery
[[Rohan]], [[Inky]], [[Iskra]], and [[Vitalii]] make it back to the church after stealing the bank, with the priest that hired them in tow. The priest comments that they're quite good at this, and asks if they would like to keep doing this. Inky considers it for a moment, but Iskra and Rohan do point out that they're busy/in the middle of a "job" at this time. The priest blesses them so that they will always be welcome and recognized by any Changebringer temples such as [[The Changebringer Lodge]].

The client that wanted the bank is a merchant by chance, and will have information with regards to the shipments that we are searching for. They talk a little more with the priest expounding on the fact that he would be very open to contracting with the group for more heists.

[[Ercari]] turns out to be the person who wanted the bank, and who might have information on the shipments. Rohan says that we believe people are being shipped - again sort of using the slave line like [[Arythas]] did at the party. Ercari comments that this is incredibly illegal and asks if we have any idea who it is. Rohan explains that the only lead we have is the name "The Lady", and that up to ten people were shipped out. He thinks about it and admits that nobody will really admit that they're buying people, but he can give entrance to the undermarket if we'd like. 

Ercari can get probably about six people entrance into the undermarket. He comments that he won't be too much help, but Rohan says that it's very helpful if we can just get into the undermarket. Ercari says that it's possible that the shipment may still be in the undermarket because The Lady may not have been able to pick things up yet. He can get us all in tomorrow, and we need to meet him at The Changebringer Lodge and he can take us there.
