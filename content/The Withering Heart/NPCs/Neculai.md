**Clan:** "Ventrue"

**Role/Title:** The Betrayer, the Defector
### Notes
A Ventrue with a unique problem: his bane requires him to drink the blood of werewolves. Because of this, he keeps a pack of werewolves as his herd, which nobody likes. Over time he has degraded into little more than a blood sucking dog - he would like *The Book of Nod* to be able to become a werewolf (or some approximation thereof) himself.

From [[content/The Withering Heart/Session Notes/Session 001|Session 001]]:
>The Librarian has been working with a Ventrue prince who has been given the unfortunate bane of werewolf blood. He no longer sees himself as one of the vampires. Having formed a herd of werewolves, he's had to deal with their politics and lives. The werewovles are formidable warriors, but they do not live the same lives, which has been changing him. He no longer identifies with Camarilla politics. He's somehow managed to get this Hecata librarian to betray his original mission and to clan Tremere, but will be giving it to this defector. His childer will show up to retrieve the book and take it back home.
