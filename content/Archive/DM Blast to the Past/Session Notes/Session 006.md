# Camp

Nymnar wanders off to an area that is within the sight of the cabin, but is relatively private, and begins to write in his journal. Damaris pulls out a piece of cloth and ties it over her eyes nervously and Pyrisou asks what is going on. Achille says not to worry about it, we'll take care of it. Pyrisou trusts Achille and we continue on.

There was mention of a village here and Pyrisou comments that we do not need to speak with the selkies as they will not help us. Silver wants to talk to the selkies just on principle, and Zimri comments that we are on a mission and we ought not to dally. Pyrisou begins to draw out a ritual in the sand of the beach. Nymnar and Zimri help with the ritual, both of them helping with the runes.

Once finished, Pyrisou chants in the water language and a huge water swims as close to the beach as it can, speaking to Pyrisou. It's called an elsewhale and Silver recalls that they can travel between planes via the oceans of the worlds.

We all begin to move through the water toward the whale. Silver helps Nymnar since he cannot swim. Damaris begins to try to drown Achille by trying to pull him to sea, which doesn't work. 
# The Elemental Plane of Water
We all board the whale and then we make it to the elemental plane of water. Pyrisou says we ought to be able to make our way from here, but if we are unlucky then we will have to hop to the prime material plane and then get to Sigil.

We are within a cave and Pyrisou explains the cave is a place that normal law-abiding citizens avoid. If we are lucky then we will be alone in here, but there might be a group currently using this cave. We will look around the cave to look around for anything useful: something we can trade, or something to give hints of the political situation of this part of the plane.

As we enter the cave we all see plenty of things. Damaris and Zimri see someone in a cage. They don't see much of the person in the cage, but he definitely looks emaciated and poorly - he's been here a long time, or something else is at play. Damaris and Zimri both approach. Zimri asks Silver to pick the lock. The cage is locked with magic.

The person inside asks what is happening and who we are, and Zimri says we are trying to get him out. He comments that we clearly aren't working for his captors. Achille asks who his captors are, and he says he won't tell us until we free him. Achille comments that this makes him not want to free us. Zimri recognizes that this is an efreet in the plane of water, and the magic on the cage is to bind his powers.

He explains he was kidnapped by faydhaan (water djinni) and he's here now. Damaris can tell that he's been here a few weeks. The ifrit comments that they've been here a few times to give him food and drink so the assumption is that he shouldn't die, but he doesn't need to be happy about living. Silver gives him some croissants and then Zimri can tell that he's deeply malnourished.

The efreet asks how we got here and Silver explains the whale, and then Silver asks how we can get out of this plane. The efreet explains that he does have a diplomatic group that he came here with that's still in the palace. The faydhaan either used magic to hide his own capture, or the rest are in prison as well. He needs to go and find out what happened to his people and save them and they will be able to get us to Sigil or most places where we would wish to go.

We get the efreet out of the cage and we go to the camp where Pyrisou is casting communication spells to try to find us a ride to Sigil.
