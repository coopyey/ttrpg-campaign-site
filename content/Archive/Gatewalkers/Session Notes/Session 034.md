# The Loot
[[Killian]] finds an accursed staff. [[Yuna]] finds grab gloves. [[Ria]] gets handwraps with crushing runes applied (handwraps of mighty blows). [[Opal]] finds a cloak of illumination, and [[Valentine]] finds a sentient sword.

The sword speaks to Valentine and he picks it up. It asks what they will do, and Valentine comments that he isn't sure and this is a little strange, as swords don't normally talk. The sword says there are enough talking weapons that they have their own god, even. The sword has forgotten its own name. 

Opal overhears Valentine talking to the sword, and Opal asks what's going on. Valentine says that he's just talking to the sword. The sword tells Valentine it's okay to let everyone know he talks, and he isn't going to try to turn Valentine away from his friends. Valentine says the sword is cool and not to worry about it. Opal asks if he talks to his weapons, and Valentine simply asks he doesn't? Opal explains he doesn't use weapons and the two of them have a little back and forth about weapons.

Valentine allows Opal to physically view the sword when Opal asks to look for it. Opal can tell the sword is magical, perhaps cursed? Opal says that he hopes it doesn't corrupt Valentine's heart, and Valentine is a little confused about it. The sword reassures once again that it's not cursed. Valentine does give the sword over to Opal, and the sword says hello to Opal.

The sword tells Opal that it has no intent on being like its sibling that's bloodthirsty and craves violence, but rather wishes to be helpful and encourage team mates to communicate better.

We all come together and then return to the dwarf looking for the sheet music. Opal lies and explains that after touching the music, there seems to be a malevolent force attached to the music and Opal's started hearing voices. The dwarf considers it and believes Opal. There's a priest/cleric from the [[Knights of Last Wall]] can review it. [[Dagon]] explains that he will put in a good word about us in his reports to the [[Aspis Consortium]]

He's wearing armor with Shelyn on it. He seems to be following the Vespine Rose - those that lost their loved ones to the Whispering One's rise. They worship both Shellyn and Calistria, so they view this passion and love as a vengeful, wrathful eye.

He's barely conscious when he grabs Valentine's hand and tells him that they need to get out there and finish the wights, and save the people.
