A city in the Hollow World - this is the deepest point anyone from the Commonwealth has ever been. This is a bit of a disjointed place - non-violent prisoners are allowed to go here and be settlers, though it is worth mentioning there *is* a prison complex and they *are* still prisoners. There is an area of non-criminal settlers, and then the prisoners + their keepers, and the two are a bit divided and disjointed in this way.

This is where we heard [[Kovar Anranios]] was being kept prisoner, as well as a possible place [[Emer Kaine]] might be.
