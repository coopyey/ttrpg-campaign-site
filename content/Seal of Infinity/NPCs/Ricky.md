**Full Name:** Ricmod Iandi

A distant cousin to [[Faylen]] and [[Beitris]]. He hates being called Ricky, but he tried to bully the twins when they were younger (and failed) so they call him that to get back at him. He's held a grudge.

He's a reporter for [[The Metropolitan Observer]], which is on the side of [[Zeth Seegmiller]] and [[Izoraxx]].
