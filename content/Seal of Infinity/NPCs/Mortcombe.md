**Full Name:** Floy Mortcombe

A young man about [[Faylen]]'s age that also went to the [[content/Seal of Infinity/Locations/Jewel|Jewel]] academy. He's the kind of guy that wants to cast spells, but doesn't think the theory behind magic is useful. Very motivated by greed, he was last seen headed back to Jewel to try to steal at least some of [[Zeth Seegmiller]]'s vast fortune left behind after his death.
