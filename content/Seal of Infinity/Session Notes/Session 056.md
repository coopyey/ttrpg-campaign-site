# Crashing the Party
[[Beitris]] and [[Faylen]] have been running interference through the party. [[Zeth Seegmiller]] is here, of course, given it's his party. He clearly doesn't want to be the center of attention here despite it being his party. He's pretty hard to read to begin with, but something about him is off - he's very flat, per se. He seems to be making calculations about everything at all times. He's dressed well, but his suit has no personality: it was something someone picked out for him.

Beitris starts double fisting the champagne at some point, and Faylen tells her to slow down. She starts to feel woozy and sits down and holds her head. Faylen realizes that there was something in the champagne. She's too woozy to actually fight with us.

Seegmiller moves and there's something wrong about it -- it takes him a moment too long, and Faylen realizes someone moves like [[Zerch]] and soon reveals himself as [[Vurg Zilardo]], demanding to know where Zeth is. Faylen happens to make direct eye contact with Zeth and, stonefaced, he crushes a crystal in his hand to teleport away. Zerch ignores the party and starts to go to where Zeth likely went.

Vurg explains that [[Kincaid]] gave them a new lease on life, yet [[Bosch]] destroyed his brother. [[Mary]] and [[Tabby]] crash through a window as the excitement begins, and Faylen teases Beitris for missing the fun after having drank too much.
