An island that has an asylum run by [[Dr. Daclaud Heinfroth]]. Heinfroth believes that to cure the ailments of the mind, one must inflict physical harm. The patients are all dramatically different versions of Heinfroth inspired by who he was at various points during his length life.

There is a volcano here.
