As we approach we see people ducking out of sight, hiding in their apartments or behind other things. The NCPD sirens are growing even louder as we see them in the distance, rapidly approaching when we make it to where the commotion is. There's an open junkyard garage that's fenced off - something easy to get into. That's where the fire fight is. 

The fence is somewhat ajar and we see movement on the other side, but we can't specifically see Phoenix. There is a building connected to the yard on the right, the door to the building is broken off of its hinges.

Emerick asks if Phoenix is okay and where she is, if she needs a medtech - she's taken a few rounds, but she's not critical. Phoenix is on the side of the yard that we are approaching from, hunkered down behind a broken down SUV. She's taken a few shots but she looks okay. Ryne starts heading to where Phoenix is, finding cover behind a broken down car about 10' away from her. He tells her that the rescue is here and it's time to head out, and she dashes for the fence without a second thought.

The rest of us approach the fence and rip the fence open for her. As we are helping her, Ryne notices one of the gangers - he doesn't recognize what gang it is - and the leader of the gang comes out with a loaded rocket launcher.
