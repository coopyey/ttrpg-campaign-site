# Saturday, October 7th, 2045
## Leviathan
The door to the south where the party was opens and [[Leviathan]], who was ransacking an office, sees a flood of light as the door opens and an alarm starts blaring right as he finishes planting a virus at the bottom of the architecture here. Before this happened, he had scrounged some documents from this office. He managed to find his own personal case file

## The Party
The party sees Leviathan come out of the office. He looks like he might have worked here, but his suit is all messed up now with wrinkles and whatnot, which isn't professional. He's in a cyberchair that has cyberlimbs with big knucks on it, which is incredibly unusual for a cyberchair.

[[Phoenix]] chimes in to say that Leviathan looks important and she wonders if he's who we're here for. Leviathan asks who we are, and Phoenix explains that we are here to loot the place and we heard a rumor of something going on here - [[Emerick]] mentions we were looking into something called Project Leviathan. As we're arguing about who's what and who, another door opens and an executive stumbles out of another room. He comments that he's been trapped for forever.

The executive asks who we all are, and then there's a scuffle as Leviathan says that the executive is coming with us. The executive isn't pleased with the idea and eventually agrees to go with us but he'll leave once we are out. The executive starts to run and [[Saint]] runs past and pushes the exec over. [[Sulfur]] handcuffs him and Emerick helps haul him up as we all run out and leave this building, running from the building as far as we can. 

As we glance behind us and get to the surface, the exec looks at Sulfur and whispers that he'll contact his driver and he needs five minutes. Leviathan and Emerick overhear this, and Leviathan springs into action to try to shock the exec. Sulfur jerks the exec away from the approaching Leviathan and tells him to stop it. He drops an EMP grenade and when the EMP goes off, the building explodes.

We realize we need to get moving quickly and the exec explains there's a bar we can go to and his driver can pick him up. We walk toward the bar and the driver comes up and tells us to uncuff the executive. Sulfur begins to uncuff him and suggests that he goes with the exec to keep him safe, and the exec agrees if it'll keep him safe.

Leviathan is ready to tackle the guy, and Sulfur pushes the man ahead. Saint tries to distract by asking about his well-being, and then Emerick starts to get between them. When Leviathan seems ready to go anyway, [[Alice]] steps in and tells him to back down. Phoenix also puts herself in between things as Saint readies a pistol in case Leviathan tries to charge.

Once the exec and Sulfur leaves, Phoenix notices Leviathan eating some papers and she takes the paper and hands it to Emerick, who reads it and can tell that it's the list of night markets as outlined above.

Night Market Dates:

- October 18th

## Sulfur & Anton Jr

He explains that the place went dark and he got stuck because of it. Sulfur asks when things went dark, and Anton explains that it's been a little over 24 hours that the place went dark and into a frenzy because his father died. He had gone in to get some documents and things went dark and the place went on lock down. Anton asks what Sulfur was doing down there.

Sulfur lies and convinces Anton that he's a gun runner. Anton believes it and comments that those that know the darker parts of the world are used to the chaos, and that there's a lot of money in this. Sulfur talks big game about connections and sets things up to be a business partner with Anton.

Sulfur asks about Leviathan and Anton explains that it's just a cyberchair with extra bells and whistles and it was going to be standard with everyone in the program. Sulfur says it's clear he's pretty pissed, and he doesn't care about what's between the two of them. Sulfur says he'll keep an eye on Leviathan and will handle him. Anton says that if Sulfur keeps him updated on information about Leviathan, he'll pay Sulfur for it.

Sulfur agrees and then gets out of the car. Once the driver goes away, he goes home and tells Jack the cat that he's got a nice in, and pets his ear, then goes out to buy a car later.
## The Party & Leviathan
Sans Sulfur, of course.

We make it to the bar and Saint buys us all some food and a round of beer. We all go to a dark corner booth that's quiet and as isolated as you can and sit down. The food comes out and we have to explain what food and eating is to Leviathan. After that, he pulls out a gun to ask us questions, and all of us freak out and tell him to put it away and to just talk to us.

He ultimately explains he's frustrated that we let the exec go. When we ask about it he has no real reason except that the exec is the only one that was alive there. He ultimately lets slip that the best way to extract information is from the dead, which has everyone absolutely stunned.

Leviathan comments that he's heard of Emerick here and there. Phoenix asks how he's heard of Emerick. He says that he heard from "him" about Emerick. Phoenix asks who that is, and Leviathan doesn't answer. Emerick comments that he imagines it's probably Juice, given the context, and Phoenix thinks that makes sense. Emerick comments that Leviathan probably knows who Ogre is as well, which he seems to recognize the name as well.

Phoenix asks if Emerick thinks it's worth it to go to the night markets, and Emerick says that he thinks that they should check them out for sure - worst case is that they just go to a night market and go shopping. There's probably something interesting there that would be worth the investigation, however.

Leviathan starts looking through his archives he's downloading and he finds the flight logs. He finds that Sazsa flew from a ship at sea to the building, and then to the band hq, then back to the building we just blew up, then to Arasaka's HQ. There's another piece of information for another location to be worked on at a later date, but that email was sent a while ago, and the facility start date was to go live on the 16th.

Alice and Phoenix leave together and Saint offers to take Leviathan back to his place for the night to rest. There's a lot of reluctance on Leviathan's end, but Saint promises there will be no cutting and it's just for the single night.

Leviathan sits up and looks to Emerick. He says that he's not done with Emerick yet and then they exchange numbers. Leviathan calls him right there to make sure the number works, and then they leave to go to Saint's place.

When everyone leaves, Emerick is alone and moves to the bar. The exec from earlier comes up to the bar and sits next to Emerick. He asks how the week's been, and Emerick says that it's been a long week. The exec says that he's had a rough week too - his father's passed and the business has gone under, but the business partners seem to be doing a lot better. Anton Jr says that his father spoke highly of Emerick because he was a man of results, he did what was asked of him without question - Anton could use someone like him. He says that just because Emerick might be good at something doesn't mean is that there is use - he asks where Emerick was the night that Juice was killed.

Emerick comments that he was there because he was living in the warehouse, he worked with the band. Anton asks if Emerick was involved, and Emerick gives a non-committal answer that it was a spectacle, hard to miss. Anton agrees that much is true, and he admits he wouldn't know what he'd do if he figured out who killed his father.

Emerick is quiet for a moment, then admits that his own father was a rather shitty person. He comments that he doesn't know what kind of relationship Anton had with Juice, but Emerick was never really able to confront his father as a kid - he ran away rather than found the strength to pull the trigger, and made sure that his father could never find him. Surely Anton could understand the complex feelings that might exist there. He pauses as he glances back down to his beer. Emerick admits that if someone where to kill his own father, he would have a rather complex mix of emotions: relief, perhaps, because his father is gone, but also anger, because it was his father to kill, you know? Anton is quiet for a moment then comments that he believes that little boy has grown up, and he's found out who killed his father.  He asks Emerick how much blood is on his hands to wind up here, and then leaves.

Seo comes in and greets Emerick enthusiastically now that the relationship doesn't have to be hidden, and Seo notices something is wrong and asks Emerick if he's okay. Emerick says that he'll explain in the car, and then Seo picks Emerick up and carries him out to the car so they can speak. The driver takes the scenic route to let them talk.

Emerick explains that he had a conversation with Juice's son and he figured out that Emerick's the one that killed Juice. Seo explains that Emerick hasn't come to terms with who he is yet, and that he knows how he got what he has. It's easy to get lost in it and relish it, but Emerick is just now facing this reality, and Anton Jr is the first one to just throw it back into his face. Seo comments that it was either Juice or Emerick that would have had to die, and that it had to wind up this way. He also thinks that Emerick had to go though this, and that little boy that was scared of his father is grown up. The two are glad that it wound up this way rather than the other, and they get back to the warehouse and go to bed.
