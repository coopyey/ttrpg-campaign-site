[[Ryne]] has grappled drummer that is the target. The drummer breaks free of the grapple. He's surrounded by everyone and the fans aren't moving to pick a fight quite yet: we are currently in control of the situation. [[Sulfur]] can tell that the fans assume this is all band beef, and the drummer is clearly perceiving everyone as a threat at this time. There's an awkward pause with tension, and the drummer pulls the hammer back on his gun and says everyone better back the fuck up.

Sulfur tries to grapple the drummer, and the drummer tries to shoot Sulfur twice, missing both shots. Sulfur gets ahold of the drummer. There's a close confrontation where Sulfur tries to shoot the drummer in the leg, but he misses, and [[Ri]] steps in with a facedown. Ri intimidates the shit out of the drummer, and the team strips the man of his weapons. They give the SMG to the fans, then drag the man out to the van. Sulfur handcuffs the man and explains the plan has changed and the new bands are changed:

The band van is now decoy with:

We pile into our vans and begin to make our way to the rendezvous point.

As Net's driving he notices a destroyed car in the middle of the road. As he's coasting down the road he tells everyone to be ready, and he notices people hanging in the buildings on the sides of the road. The members look to be Maelstrom. For a brief second Net notices the flash of a sniper's scope in the light, and then he feels the car start to swerve as a tire pops and spirals out of control. Net struggles with it for a moment, but regains control of the vehicle and continues to drive.

Net attempts a warning suppressive fire and manages to suppress one guy, and continues to drive as fast as he can. Seo texts Emerick to let him know that they are under fire with the Maelstrom, and then sends the location to Emerick. Emerick relays the information to Powder, and Powder begins to speed toward the area where Net is.

The Maelstrom manage to disable Net's car and they get out, scrambling to deal with the Maelstrom before them. With quick thinking, Saint throws an EMP grenade and disables two of the Maelstrom by making their cyberarms useless. Combat continues and Net's car is rendered inoperable.

Sulfur calls for backup from his team and someone named Copper calls back and confirms that he and one other can head over.

Combat continues.

Combat ends and Sulfur immediately points at one of his reinforcements and they head into the building to wrangle the drummer that managed to get free from the van during the combat. Sulfur finds him in a storage room and the man immediately says that he doesn't know what the fuck Sulfur wants, but he'll give him anything. Sulfur says to hand over the money now, which he does. Sulfur then shoots the man's leg and breaks it, and then shoves his gun in the drummer's face and demands where the rest of the band is.

The drummer caves immediately and gives his friends up: they rehearse in Heywood. He's new to everything - he doesn't know what they did to him. He quickly scribbles down the address for their rehearsal. As Sulfur takes the note, he hears a beeping noise as the drummer has some expression that looks smug, as if he's going to take Sulfur down with him. Sulfur realizes that the drummer has an explosive implanted into him that was from the band, and it has malfunctioned so it does not explode. There's a moment that he is surprised, and then he keels over as blood floods out of his body: the bomb didn't explode properly, but it destroyed all of his internal organs.

Sulfur finds a piece of glass and carves the word Penny into his chest, then looks to Copper and his friend and asks them to take the corpse to the address the drummer wrote down. They take the body and go to take care of things.

Powder tears around a corner and we come to a screeching halt in the street. We all rush out of the van with guns drawn, ready for action. Net confirms that the action is over and Emerick immediately goes to check on Seo as Net retrieves his heavy pistol. Net asks if Sulfur got his guy, and when Sulfur confirms, Net asks what compensation he gets. Sulfur offers to help fix his van, and Net wants money.

Net figures out that Sulfur is acting weird, and Net asks if Sulfur's a cop. Sulfur says he's worse: world police. Charley steps up and clarifies that he's CIA. Net says that he doesn't care about Sulfur being a fed, and he deserves fair compensation. Ri steps up and says he was promised drugs. Sulfur lays the blame at Ryne's feet, and Net agrees that Ryne fucked up.

Charley steps in between them and tells Sulfur to come clean about this. Sulfur says that he used to have a wife and a kid and some guys took up issue with his wife. They broke in and some shots were fired - Sulfur took one of their arms, but when he woke up his wife was gone, and his daughter was dead. He just wants his wife back.

Charley tells Net that he now knows why we are here, and then asks Net how much it will cost to repair the van. Net takes some time and comes up with a quote, explaining the costs. Charley sends money over to Net to take care of it, and then we all pile into he car and head back to the warehouse.

Ryne dissociates completely and his blood runs red as he no longer wishes to live. He uses his chain ripper to attack himself until he falls to zero hit points. Juice says that the next person to threaten anyone in the band will meet the same fate.

He then approaches Sulfur and says that he made a deal with someone in the band. He explains that the band helped him with a job and Sulfur put up a reward he can't even hold up. Sulfur starts to argue that he can get the kilo of coke, and Juice attempts to slap him. Sulfur manages to dodge it and Ogre comes crashing toward him, hitting him twice. As Sulfur leaves to get the kilo of cocaine, the tension in the room is very palpable. Emerick is not let off the hook immediately. Juice actually looks back to Emerick and tells him to follow. Emerick follows Juice and Ogre to the office, with Ri coming along as well. Everyone else returns to their business as before.

When we get into the office and sit, Ogre shuts the door. Juice asks Ri how much Sulfur owes him, and Ri says he thinks it was a kilo - Emerick confirms that amount. Juice pulls a kilo out and hands it to Ri, and says that if it's gone in a week, then they will have a talk. Juice isn't giving it lightly: Ri owes him for this. It stays with him for a month. He won't let Ri wash up like a piece of trash. He's giving it to Ri for fun, not to fuck up his life. Juice then tells Ri to get the fuck out of his sight. Without hesitation, Ri grabs the kilo and leaves.

Once Ri's gone, Juice looks to Emerick and begins to speak. He says that there's an awful lot going on in this world, and a lot of people are looking at Emerick and wanting his help with work, and looking out for him. Emerick's making a name for himself out there: doing work for his friends and helping them break other people out, costing Juice people in the process - all for what, some story? Juice remembers when he was a blogger looking for a quick buck. He knew how to hold a gun and use it, but now corporations are breathing down his neck, dangling promises in front of him, like the 141,175 eds he has.

Emerick's face goes pale and he looks up at Juice in disbelief, confused as to how Juice knows about that. Juice explains that it's easy to know when it happened: [[Cipher]] hurt a band member, and they were punished. Torrent and Emerick were good, yes, and didn't have to deal with the addictive substances, but they were out for quite some time - time enough for Emerick to have some toys installed into him. Juice knows how to protect his assets, and any problems end up six feet under.

Emerick stammers out that Juice knows everything and has known everything, and Seo puts together that it was after the punishment for Cipher slapping [[Alice]]. Emerick just repeats it a few times and Seo admits he's at a loss and isn't sure how to fix it, and Emerick isn't sure it can be fixed.

The two fall silent and Emerick leans up against Seo, only wanting some some comfort, and Seo pulls him close. Before the two can get comfortable, Juice opens Emerick's door and tells Seo to meet him in the office. Seo gets up to go and Emerick manages to grab his hand, staring up at Seo as if asking him to stay. Seo just reaches the decision to stay before Ogre comes in and grabs him by the shoulder to move him to Juice's office after all.
