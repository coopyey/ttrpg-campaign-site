## The Party: Upstairs

[[Saint]] and [[Sulfur]] look over the corpses scattered about the first floor and it's clear that they all died via a gunshot at the back of the head. Saint and Sulfur confirm this and Sulfur isn't sure if it's a ready room or a doomsday cult or something like that. They both continue to review and figure out that they're all staff members. Sulfur rolls one body over and sees a logo on it: a digital font that is in lines and dots to make a symbol. The symbol ultimately makes an B with some additional details around it to distort it. It's something obscure, not really known outside of Russia.

Sulfur has been to Russia and recognizes the logo. The logo is for the company вознесение (English: Ascension). Sulfur didn't deal too much with them but he recalls them from some old Soviet archives, they're kind of like their boogeyman. It doesn't really rule out a cult. [[Alice]] peeks her head around and says that [[Juice]] is not someone who's superstitious, so a cult doesn't make sense, but maybe their operations are cult-adjacent? She just never really got the religious vibe from him.

Sulfur found a red keycard, and Saint finds a green keycard.

There's a little more back and forth about that and the fact that the stairs are barricaded is mentioned. Sulfur does comment that they had tried to destroy this place and then something was barricaded downstairs
## The Party: Managerial Offices & Staff Area
We head downstairs and find a door that requires a blue keycard. We move on to another door and swipe the red keycard. We swipe the card and it starts to open then stops, then slowly starts to open. It takes a full minute to open. As we enter this area, the lights are flickering, with spots of pitch blackness. Emerick pulls his flashlight out and they start to move through this area.

This lower area is clearly offices where the staff mostly existed. We do find a clock with 10 minutes stuck on it, frozen.

There are more bodies in this area, all killed with a bullet to the back of the head just like upstairs.

He had killed himself as shown by the bullet wound at the bottom of his head and a pistol still in his hand. Sulfur takes the pistol after checking how much ammunition is in it.

### The Holding Room
We leave the security office with SMGs and pistols with tactical lights so that Emerick isn't the only one with a flash light. There's a door that leads down and then a door across the way. The stairwell is dark, and the door across from us is locked with no key card. When we peek into the door it seems to be a holding cell.

Alice punches the dead bolt on the door and Emerick hesitates to get behind Sulfur in case there's anything hostile on the other side. He flashes the flash light inside and we all cautiously enter the room to look inside. There's a series of four hospital beds on the right side. Two of the beds have a body on them, and a dozen bodies on the floor besides. The people that were in here were fighting to not be killed.

A staff member was clearly killed by the fighting and it's clear everyone died in a different way. There is a reception desk at the far end of the room and an office room behind it - this seems to be a secondary intake and assessment area.

Whatever was in here went wrong.

Emerick is looking up as we move through and he finally notices a body in the ceiling. Saint pulls the body down and there's a heavy metallic thud - a Maelstrom body. The sound causes a body on one of the beds jump. They're confused and asks what's going on and there's confusion. Sulfur gives fake names and the person in the bed introduces himself as Ronnie.

Ronnie was down on his luck and he could have some work and be part of something, but he has no clue how he got here. We discuss back and forth and Phoenix pays him to describe who brought him here and it was Juice. Of course it was Juice. We go into the office and there's mostly just intake forms. Emerick sifts through them and gets curious and starts to go back a while, skimming everything, knowing it *has* to be here. For the best that his memory can remember, he goes back to when he and the OG crew should have been admitted here, and none of our papers are in here. It's clear that Emerick grows more agitated and Saint asks what's wrong. Emerick eventually responds that he's looking for certain paperwork, and when Phoenix asks, he comments that Torrent should have paperwork. Phoenix comments that perhaps Juice snuck certain people in, and that seems to bother Emerick more.

We go down 3 flights of stairs before we enter a small hallway. There's another door with cryosystems inside, where there may be some people still inside. There's also an experimentation room.

We start with the cryosystems. One system is no longer functional and it seems the person inside is now dead, and the system had just failed. The other two are still currently running. Saint, Phoenix, and Alice are in here.

Very experimental. Its gloves are currently bloodied, and a subject on the mat that looks to have no head - currently paste.
