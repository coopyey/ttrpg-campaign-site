## The Venue
They make it to the venue and they meet the bartender. She's very interesting: she has mantis legs and is around 8' tall, with cyberarms that are elongated to match her height. She goes into one of her offices and replaces her limbs to speak with us on a more even level and so she's not so intimidating. Her "regular" cyberlimbs are a very sleek bronze color, and also very slender framed, like the rest of her. They are very elegant.

Her name is **Alva**.

She welcomes us and tells us where to set up. If we need anything, she does have things being stocked up in the backstage rooms. If there's anything we need that's unavailable, she will help us out the best she can. Charley talks to her most of the time since Juice is not yet present.
