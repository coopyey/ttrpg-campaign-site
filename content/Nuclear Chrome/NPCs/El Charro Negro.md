**Full Name:** Javier Osorio-Gutierrez

**Alias:**  El Charro Negro

[[Net]]'s uncle that was in jail until Net posted bail. Deeply involved in the [[Santo Domingo Duendecitos]] leadership in some fashion, though Net very much wants him to quit the gang life.

Described by Net as a harsh man that has always been unpleasant. Net explicitly told Emerick that working for Javier is the same as working for Juice - neither place is a safe place, and he'd be better off elsewhere.
# Group Interactions
In [[content/Nuclear Chrome/Session Notes/Session 037|Session 036]] he bailed Net, Saint, and Emerick out of the sticky situation they had found themselves in after Omen and Garf died to the hands of Mr. Steele in a forgotten government bunker. In [[Session 038]] he outlined the terms of this: they are welcome to stay under his protection, but if they stay, they must work for him. He had bailed them out once (1) and he will *not* be doing that again if they leave right now.
