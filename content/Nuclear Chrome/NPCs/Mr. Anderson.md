A member of the Night City equivalent of the FBI, this is a contact that [[Emerick]] has known for a while. Mr. Anderson had come across one of Emerick's stories and read it and was interested. The two established something of a beneficial relationship - if Mr. Anderson needed help on a case but couldn't get past the red tape he would ask Emerick, and if Emerick couldn't find something he might ask Mr. Anderson for his resources.
