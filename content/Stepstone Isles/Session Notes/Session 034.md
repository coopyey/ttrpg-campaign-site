# Kruthik Battle
Combat continues. We focus on the hive lord and all come together to attack it and try to end this once and for all. After a long and grueling fight with far too much acid, Lothar manages to kill the hive lord. We clean up the rest of the kruthiks around here and move into the large chamber and we are greeted with the smell of carrion. We find the dead people, horses, and dogs in here. We find every unaccounted for body at least in part here (other than the changeling). We do not see the fake baby anywhere. There are numerous soft, ridged, bowling ball to basketball sized turquoise balls that are nestled near the carrion pile.

[[Ships]] looks at the eggs and they starting to lose structural integrity and squirm as we pick the eggs up - it's clear they're about to hatch. We move quickly to get the eggs together so that we can destroy them with a combination of the explosive barrel and moon beam. We take care of this easily and the eggs are destroyed.

When it's all said and done, we discuss what to do with the corpses. We decide to move them outside of the cave and cremate them.
