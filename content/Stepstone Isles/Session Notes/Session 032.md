# The Fallen Castle
The castle had fallen into the earth. [[Sarras]] can tell that there is sandstone that had melted into mud, which implies acid was used to aid the castle falling, but there's no other sign of magic. [[Ships]] finds 25 people corpses, 19 horses, 3 ponies, 14 mastiffs, and 4 wolfhounds - this means we are missing: 15 people, 5 dogs, and 6 horses. Ships also notices the crushed remains of what he can tentatively call fifth or sixth largest (that is, smaller than fourth largest), and we locate the musicians that they were playing when this happened. Neither sister nor the baby are here, but we do see the priest Renia, we do not see the mage. Almost all of the armsmen and most of the servants are here, but not the cook, the maids, the stablemaster, or the houndmaster. We also do not see the corpses of the Baron or his wife (who were murdered previously).

As a reminder: they speak through telepathy, and spoke in a language we don't know so Ships had to use comprehend languages as [[Calthel]] was not there.
