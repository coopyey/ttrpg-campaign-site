One of the guards at the Abbey that [[Sarras]] is from.

OOC Notes:
>*Human. A paladin candidate who finished training at the same time as Sarras, but was never sent out alone. While he is blessed like any paladin, he is very intense and ferocious to the point of being almost feral - almost but not quite a berserker. He is often sent out on missions that call for a larger group or when he can be supervised.*
