- Temple of Eight in town
- Henge NE of town for outdoor rituals
- Three accessible beaches
	- NE - safest and used the most
	- SE - boathouse and drydock for fishermen
	- NW - bat cave and guano mine
- Biggest harvester of guano for spells
- There is a tree grove to the east
- The island is 600 ft up, with the lighthouse being an additional 200 ft
- Expected threats
	- Bandits from Landbarrow (not an issue for years)
	- Aberrations from the sea, especially associated with Dagon
	- Wyverns are a possibility, but unlikely

The overall island is craggy and is generally inhospitable-looking.
