**Island:** [[Aberly]]

The seat of [[Marquis]] Petra.

This is built in two halves over a chasm and is easily defensible. There are no windows on the second story except for a large gold stained glass window tot he audience chamber, but windows do exist on the third and fourth stories. Notably, no bides exist here thanks to Petra's pet peryton, Furcival.
