Before where you stand appears a  dragonborn wreathed in turquoise flame so that the color of its scales cannot be seen, lacking arms but with between four and eight long tendrils of wavering flame licking out sinuously from its shoulders, appears in the room. Despite the pallid glow of flames, it is strangely hard to see, as if your eyes slide off of it.

Standing before you, it coughs up a brilliant, sparkling ember of cyan and white, holds it out to you with two tendrils, and, making contact with the burnt-out husks of its eyes, swallows performatively.

It wants you to eat it.

When you do, you find yourself standing on an undulant surface of speckled aquamarine flame, as though fire could be an ocean.  Your whole body prickles with the feeling of electricity, and you see several of the scales on your arms loosen and fall away, shedding out of season.

Within the flame, you see visions.

White crocodiles with rams’ horns curling past their jaws and human faces staring up from atop their heads crawl about on a frozen pond, while giant olive-green eyes buoy up beneath the surface of the ice, staring at them.

A forest of beech trees - you recognize, distantly, Noduliande, the northern woods of Compass Tor - is filled with sulfurous yellow torches, threatening the trees.

You see the shadow of the Lodestone Tower inside the navigator’s school judder and spin erratically as days and nights flash by - it is not the smooth procession of the seasons, but an erratic whirl of jealous shadow, whirling first one way, then another, the shadow stretching out to mouth against the snake-riddled foundations of the other towers.

A dark, gnarled oak tree, dug up from the ground and sprawles upon its roots on a bleak and barren field, glows with a sea-green light, inviting and lush.  Serpents coil around the roots of the tree, gnawing at it.

Then you are back in your bed, feeling the ember caught and smoldering in your throat.  Take 5 fire damage, after regaining HP from rest. You will be unable to speak until some of this damage is healed, and unable to speak above a whisper until all of this damage is healed.  There is a large scorch mark where the emissary of Fayruz stood.

While much of the vision is still obscure to you, you understand this much instinctively:
- The dragonborn of the Stepstone Isles are being seen by the Sleeping Elders, especially Sochet, with unusual clarity, for unknown reasons
- Something threatening is in the forest of Noduliande
- Fayruz wishes you to clear the Navigators’ School free from the influences of any warlock patron except itself
- On the island of Duffholm, an ally of Fayruz is threatened

You gain the following bonus invocation:

Guiding Star
1×/long rest, bonus action:
Until the end of your next turn, you have advantage on all skill checks and disadvantage on all saving throws. You may spend a warlock spell slot as a free action to regain a use of this ability.
